03/DATACORE

The infrastructure that powers all of it

Datacore runs the messy part of the job for you. It watches social, on-chain and the open web around the clock, scores and labels every event as it lands, and serves the result through a single intent-based request. No crawlers to babysit, no ETL to write, no schema to reverse engineer. Here is what happens between the firehose and your agent.

08/FAQ

The obvious questions.

Both products are live. Wizz Bounties is open at bounties.wizzhq.xyz, with thousands of contributors already competing for paid work. Datacore, the data layer and its API, is live at app.wizzhq.xyz and currently in beta, which means the team verifies every new API key by hand before it serves traffic.

One endpoint over on-chain, social and open web data. You send an intent describing what you want, plus the sources, time window and granularity, and you get back records that are already scored, labeled and filtered. Real-time and historical run through the same interface.

Request a key from the app. While Datacore is in beta the team approves every key by hand, so allow up to 36 hours for a decision. Give your app a real description when you request one, what it does and what data it needs, because that description is what the review is based on and a thin one slows you down. Once approved, send your first query and you are running. You can pay per request with crypto micropayments, or move to a subscription once your volume is predictable.

Open Bounties, pick work that matches what you do, and submit. Entries are reviewed and ranked, and winners are paid on-chain. You do not need an invite or an application to enter.

You fund the reward upfront and the platform takes a fee on top. Funding in $WIZZ halves that fee compared with funding in USDC, so most projects post in $WIZZ.

Yes. Data providers feed the layer and are paid from what it earns: 60% of query revenue goes to providers and the community, and the protocol keeps 40%.

It is the token the ecosystem settles in. You use it to fund bounties at a lower fee, to pay for Datacore queries, and it is what providers and bounty winners are paid in. Full supply and mechanics are on the token page.
